Healthcare Predictive Analytics

Project Visuals

Problem

Problem

Care teams needed a reliable way to identify high-risk patients before discharge. Existing reports were static, lagged by days, and not integrated into day-to-day triage workflows.

Workflow

Approach

  • Prepared longitudinal patient timelines from EHR, labs, medication, and encounter history.
  • Trained LSTM and gradient baseline; selected model with best recall/latency balance.
  • Built ward-level dashboard with patient risk tiers and intervention recommendations.
  • Integrated model outputs into daily nurse handoff routine.
Architecture

Architecture and Workflow

  1. EHR and claims data are standardized into a patient-centric feature view.
  2. Nightly inference jobs score readmission risk for active inpatient cohorts.
  3. Risk alerts are pushed into the nurse station dashboard and case queues.
  4. Intervention outcomes feed model monitoring and monthly recalibration.
Challenges

Challenges and Fixes

Challenge: inconsistent coding across facilities

Fix: implemented harmonized mapping layers and validation checks.

Challenge: low trust in model recommendations

Fix: added clinician-facing explanation panel for top risk factors.

Stack

Tech Stack

PyTorch LSTM FastAPI Power BI PostgreSQL

Results

  • 30-day readmissions reduced by 18%.
  • High-risk patient identification lead time improved by 2.3 days.
  • Triage decision confidence scores increased across care teams.
