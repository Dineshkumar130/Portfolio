Problem

Problem

The customer success team had no early warning system for churn risk, and interventions were happening too late. Leadership needed interpretable reasons behind risk predictions, not just scores.

Workflow

Approach

  • Built account-level features from product usage, support tickets, billing, and NPS.
  • Compared gradient boosting and neural models; selected best calibrated model.
  • Added SHAP explanations to show top churn drivers per account.
  • Delivered risk segments in weekly action dashboard for success managers.
Architecture

Architecture and Workflow

  1. Daily event and account snapshots land in the analytical warehouse.
  2. Feature job aggregates engagement, support, and billing signals.
  3. Model batch-scoring writes risk tiers back into CRM and dashboard.
  4. Customer success playbooks trigger retention actions by risk segment.
Challenges

Challenges and Fixes

Challenge: poor recall for enterprise accounts

Fix: added segment-aware feature interactions and tier-specific sampling.

Challenge: low adoption of model output by CS team

Fix: added SHAP driver explanations directly in weekly workflows.

Tech Stack

Results

  • Overall churn dropped by 25% in two quarters.
  • Risk-model precision at top decile improved to 72%.
  • Customer success intervention SLA improved by 37%.
